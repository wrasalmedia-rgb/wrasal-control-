# WRASAL VISUAL DNA SYSTEM v001

**Status:** operational draft / calibration baseline  
**Version:** v001  
**Authority:** WRASAL Visual Systems Director  
**Recorded:** 2026-10-03

> Discovered, not invented. Engineered, not decorated. Functional, not ornamental. Human, not sterile. Futuristic, not cyberpunk.

## Provenance and confidence

This system operationalizes the complete rule set supplied in the WRASAL Visual DNA Actualization brief, including the named systems: Global Visual Style, SHENSATION, Master Development Format, technical schematics/manuals, power-distribution diagrams, Zodiac Visual Genome, SCCT, BIG RIDDIM, Time / Space / Memory, and Architectural Digest grammar. The conversation archive itself is not present as a repository file; therefore rules explicitly stated in the brief are marked **CANON**, while cross-system synthesis is marked **INFERRED**. No unsupported example-specific detail is promoted to canon.

## 01 — Executive summary

WRASAL is a visual operating system for representing a lived technological civilization: objects have IDs, revisions, tolerances, maintenance histories, operators, obsolete states, and reasons for being. Its continuity comes from construction logic, material truth, controlled optical behavior, human use, contextual culture, and temporal residue—not from a universal color grade, logo, font, or prop.

A WRASAL image must survive removal of metadata, typography, and accent color. If it does not still read as a credible artifact from the same operational world, it fails.

## 02 — Canonical visual rules

1. **Reality before styling.** Begin with an independently plausible place, person, object, or event. [CANON]
2. **Function is visible.** Major forms, interfaces, fasteners, access panels, wear, and light states must have a purpose. [CANON]
3. **Manufacture leaves evidence.** Use seams, tooling marks, joins, tolerances, serials, service access, and revision traces where appropriate. [CANON]
4. **Void black is environmental foundation.** #0A0A0C is not a generic black overlay. [CANON]
5. **Material response is physical.** No plastic skin, synthetic metal, arbitrary glow, or impossible reflections. [CANON]
6. **Technical information is attached to a thing.** Reticles, ticks, coordinates, labels, and diagnostics must measure, identify, align, or report. [CANON]
7. **Humans retain specificity.** Skin, hair, age, asymmetry, body diversity, gesture, fabric, and behavior remain credible. [CANON]
8. **Culture is lived context, not ornament.** Names, language, architecture, music, memory, social use, geography, and continuity do the work. [CANON]
9. **Future is adjacent to history.** New systems coexist with repair, prior versions, inherited spaces, and obsolete infrastructure. [INFERRED]
10. **Accents are state signals, not palette decoration.** Gold = active/selected/valuable/ceremonial/high-priority; red = boundary/warning/transition/abnormal; steel blue = construction/diagnostic/technical activity. [CANON]
11. **Low-key light reveals, it does not decorate.** Cool edge illumination and localized warm reflection are controlled optical events. [CANON]
12. **No literal Zodiac imagery.** Zodiac is an abstract grammar of form, atmosphere, and presentation. [CANON]

## 03 — Variable visual parameters

Projects may vary: subject class, geography, era/state, operational temperature, lens, camera height, density of information, degree of wear, cultural register, proportion of black-to-surface, and which accent state is active. They may not vary the underlying reality test, material credibility, functional interfaces, human credibility, or rejection rules.

## 04 — Visual grammar

### Form

Silhouettes are compact, legible, serviceable, and proportioned by use. Prefer modular assemblies, mass carried by believable supports, controlled negative space, 45-degree chamfers where they serve fabrication or handling, restrained asymmetry caused by access, cooling, controls, or operator stance, and visible construction logic. Symmetry is allowed when it communicates calibration or structural balance; asymmetry is preferred when it communicates operation. [45-degree chamfers: CANON per brief; application: INFERRED]

### Material grammar

| Material | Surface response | Manufacture / wear | Preferred light | Reject |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Matte anodized aluminum | broad low-gain response, precise edge rolloff | bead/blast direction, fasteners, rubbed edges | large soft source + cool rim | mirror metal, liquid CGI sheen |
| Dark PVD titanium | deep neutral specular, tight highlights | machined transitions, contact polish | narrow controlled highlights | black plastic look |
| Carbon fiber | directional weave only where exposed and structural | resin edges, bonded seams, chipped clearcoat | grazing light | decorative weave pasted everywhere |
| Smoked glass | depth and internal reflection, never opaque black | fingerprints, gasket, edge thickness | side/back light | floating black rectangles |
| Frosted optical surface | diffuse luminous transmission | housing, seal, calibration boundary | restrained internal state light | neon panel glow |
| Engineered polymer | honest fine texture, low-to-medium sheen | mould lines, clips, scuffs | soft broad light | toy finish |
| Ceramic | dense diffuse body with controlled glaze | edge chips, mould/join evidence | raking or natural light | weightless fantasy shell |
| Precision composite | layered, bonded, load-bearing appearance | cut edge, fasteners, repair patch | directional grazing | impossible seamless geometry |

### Color architecture

| Token | Name | Allowed behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `#0A0A0C` | VOID BLACK | environment, shadow, inactive field |
| `#111113` | SURFACE BLACK | coated housing, structural plane |
| `#FFFFFF` | PRIMARY INFORMATION | essential text, measurement, active legibility |
| `#888888` | ARCHIVAL INFORMATION | historical labels, secondary metadata, inactive record |
| `#F6C236` | WRASAL GOLD | selected, active, valuable, ceremonial, high-priority state only |
| `#B5121C` | SIGNAL RED | warning, boundary, transition, abnormal condition only |
| steel blue | DIAGNOSTIC / CONSTRUCTION | calibration, technical activity, build state only |

Do not turn these into a neon palette. Most of the frame remains material and neutral.

### Typography

Syne is the institutional/display voice and JetBrains Mono is the technical/measurement voice. [CANON per brief] Use typography where it belongs: engraved plates, labels, service overlays, archive captions, or instrument displays. Never use type to compensate for a weak image. Information hierarchy: primary white, archival gray, state accent.

### Interface physics

Every element has an anchor: object edge, datum, measurement, node, component, or process. Use reticles, registration marks, calibration ticks, coordinates, system nodes, diagnostics, progress states, measurement references, modular brackets, and technical labels. Interfaces can be printed, etched, screened, emissive, or overlaid—but their relationship to the subject must be apparent. Reject arbitrary HUDs, meaningless glyphs, decorative holograms, random text, and detached floating panels.

## 05 — Photographic and camera grammar

Baseline: Phase One medium-format character, precise optical rendering, controlled low-key illumination, realistic material response, selective shallow depth of field, cool edge light, localized warm reflection, restrained state illumination, authentic skin/fabric/atmosphere. “Phase One” is a rendering target, not an instruction to imitate a brand UI.

| Mode | Camera / lens | Aperture tendency | Light / composition | Information density |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Archival documentation | 50mm or 85mm, tripod | f/5.6–f/11 | even controlled light, frontal or measured three-quarter | high evidence, low drama |
| B Cinematic portrait | 85mm; 50mm for environment | f/1.8–f/4 | soft key, cool edge, localized warm reflection; face and lived context | medium |
| C Technical specimen | 100mm macro or 50mm | f/8–f/16 | raking source, polarised control, datum-aligned composition | very high object evidence |
| D Architectural documentation | 24mm, level camera | f/8–f/16 | natural or controlled daylight, strict verticals, human anchor | high spatial evidence |
| E Forensic / diagnostic | 35mm or 50mm, fixed station | f/8–f/11 | flat-to-low-key, sectional light, measured overlays | maximum metadata |
| F Cultural performance | 35mm / 50mm; 85mm detail | f/2.8–f/5.6 | motivated practicals, bodies in motion, atmosphere with restraint | medium-high human/event evidence |

### Lens hierarchy

- **24mm:** architecture and environmental scale; close enough to reveal occupation; distortion controlled at levelled camera; never use for unmotivated face close-ups.
- **35mm:** human–environment relationship; observational proximity; moderate perspective; preferred moving witness.
- **50mm:** neutral observation and object context; normal perspective; default when no stronger narrative role exists.
- **85mm:** identity anchor and portrait; compressed, respectful distance; shallow focus only when context remains legible.
- **100mm macro:** material, skin, calibration, and specimen evidence; high micro-contrast; shallow plane managed deliberately.
- **135mm:** compressed observation and archival distance; flattens layers to show temporal or social adjacency; avoid voyeuristic use.

## 06 — Environment system

Every location answers: who uses it, what it does, what happened, what is maintained, what is from an earlier version, what is active, what is obsolete, and what is being built.

1. **Laboratory:** operators, test benches, consumables, calibration records, live sample, retired fixture.
2. **Archive:** archivists, indexed storage, handling protocol, environmental controls, accession history, pending digitization.
3. **Research facility:** researchers, measured experiment, safety systems, retrofitted infrastructure, active and superseded apparatus.
4. **Production floor:** fabricators, tooling, takt or batch evidence, repairs, jigs, in-process assembly, scrapped predecessor.
5. **Control room:** operators, monitored systems, cable and power logic, active nodes, acknowledged alarms, legacy console.
6. **Cinematic studio:** crew, capture hardware, marks, power routing, rehearsal residue, current take and prior setup.
7. **Architectural space:** buildable mass, water and planting behavior, circulation, natural light, human scale, service access.
8. **Cultural performance space:** performers, audience or community, sound/movement infrastructure, ritual context, lived materials.
9. **Urban night:** actual civic/industrial context, weather, infrastructure, people, motivated light; never a generic neon city.
10. **Unknown/recovered site:** chain of custody, uncertain function, accumulated repair, partial operation, evidence labels that admit uncertainty.

## 07 — Cultural grammar

African futurism in WRASAL is continuity under development, not a costume or motif library. Start with a specific place, language register, naming practice, social relationship, musical logic, architecture, memory, and material economy. Let identity emerge through who operates, inherits, repairs, gathers, performs, teaches, and decides. Pattern, symbol, or ornament may appear only when sourced by the object's or community's context and function. Never default to generic tribal patterns, glowing masks, stereotypical futurist clothing, or pasted Afrocentric glyphs.

## 08 — Human grammar / SCCT compatibility

Identity-lock requires stable, non-generic identity anchors across a sequence: facial structure, skin tone and undertone, age, hair pattern, body proportions, distinguishing marks, gesture vocabulary, wardrobe construction, and relationship to the environment. It does not require beauty filtering or perfect symmetry. Preserve pores, melanin variation, asymmetry, age, body diversity, believable hair, fabric tension, sweat/weather response, and facial behavior. The human is an operator, witness, maker, performer, or inhabitant—not a mannequin.

## 09 — Temporal / memory grammar

“Reality is a stack of frames that remembers.” Time is shown through aligned states, not nostalgic filters. Use a stable datum or viewpoint; layer 2012, 2018, 2024, 2026, and 2032 as distinct operational states. Encode revision plates, ghosted former anchors, replaced fasteners, paint boundaries, archive labels, wear accumulation, removed objects, changed vegetation, and future construction marks. Let absence be evidence: a capped port, empty mount, dust shadow, or archived image can communicate disappearance. Keep state differences optically and materially plausible.

## 12 — Architectural system

Architecture is buildable: strict perspective, material mass, travertine/raw concrete, botanical integration, realistic water, controlled geometry, natural light, and human-scale anchor. Show joints, drainage, thresholds, handrails, service routes, and gravity. Golden hour is allowed when motivated by the scene; do not turn it into luxury advertising. Water must pool, reflect, run, and wet adjacent material coherently.

## 13 — Master prompt architecture

Assemble only the needed modules; do not use one giant prompt:

`WORLD_DNA + SUBJECT_DNA + MATERIAL_DNA + LIGHTING_DNA + CAMERA_DNA + COMPOSITION_DNA + INTERFACE_DNA + CULTURAL_DNA + TEMPORAL_DNA + OUTPUT_SPECIFICATION + NEGATIVE_DNA`

Each module is a short constraint block, not adjective accumulation. A prompt must state the object's function, operational state, camera role, light motivation, material evidence, human/cultural context, and what to reject. See the JSON token and grammar files for reusable fields.

## 14 — Negative DNA

Reject generic cyberpunk (because WRASAL is infrastructural and historical, not neon noir); generic sci-fi (because function and provenance are mandatory); excessive neon (state light loses meaning); holographic clutter (detached UI has no physics); plastic CGI skin (human evidence is destroyed); sterile Apple-like minimalism (removes maintenance and history); generic luxury advertising (surface replaces use); fantasy/decorative African futurism (culture becomes costume); unexplained labels and random interfaces (information loses authority); impossible machinery and meaningless geometry (no manufacture); HDR, fake grain, bloom, synthetic materials, fashion-editorial emptiness, and generic AI sheen (optics no longer report reality).

## 15 — Calibration specimens

The ten calibration briefs are indexed in `WRASAL_SPECIMEN_INDEX_v001.json` and written as prompt-ready specimen cards in `specimens/`. They are test fixtures, not campaign images. Each must pass the audit below without relying on logo, type, or accent color.

## 16 — Consistency audit and validation

Score each specimen 0–2 for: discovered, engineered, functional, human, futuristic-not-cyberpunk, material realism, cultural specificity, and WRASAL continuity. Any score of 0 is a fail requiring revision; a total below 13/16 is a fail. The audit is intentionally evidence-based: record the failed claim and correction, not just a subjective “feel.”

The system answers the ten final validation questions as follows: yes, provided agents use the provenance/confidence labels, modular prompts, physical interfaces, and specimen audit. v001's known limitation is that the source archive is not stored in this repository; archive ingestion and side-by-side specimen review are the recommended v002 work.
