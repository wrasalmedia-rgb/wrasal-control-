# A 25-Minute Situation Exercise

You do not need any background for this exercise. You will look at one unresolved situation in a structured way and write a few short answers.

Choose something real but **not urgent or high-risk**. Do not use an active crisis, safety issue, medical emergency, or legal emergency. You may skip any question or stop at any time.

Please do not write your name or detailed private information on this sheet. A short private label is enough.

## What you need

- about 25 minutes;
- a pen or a place to type; and
- one situation that is still unresolved.

There are no right answers. It is completely okay if nothing changes.

## 2. Work with the situation — about 10 minutes

### A. Keep noticing separate from meaning

Read your list of things you can point to. Add one more if needed. For this step, do not explain what they mean yet.

### B. Name the meaning you are making

Finish this sentence:

> “Given those things, the meaning I am making is …”

> ______________________________________________

### C. Make room for one other possible reading

Write one other possible reading of the same situation. It does not need to be positive or correct. It only needs to be plausible enough to consider.

> ______________________________________________

### D. Choose, defer, or gather information

Given both readings, what is one small and reversible action you could take in the next 24 hours?

You may instead choose to wait, gather information, or take no action. Name that choice plainly.

## 4. One follow-up — about 2 minutes now

If you consent to one follow-up, choose an existing way for the facilitator to contact you in about 24 hours. Do not put contact details on this sheet.

The follow-up asks only:

1. Did you take the action, choose not to take it, or change it?
2. What did you directly notice afterward?
3. What remains unknown?

You may decline the follow-up or decline any question. No response is also information to record accurately.

## Finish

Tell the facilitator that you are finished, skipped a section, or stopped. You do not need to explain your answers or the larger system behind this exercise.
