# BUILD-002 — WRAYvolution Encounter Specification

## Purpose

Prepare one small, facilitator-led encounter that one consenting participant can complete in **25 minutes** (never more than 30 minutes) without learning the underlying WRASAL or WRAYvolution architecture.

The encounter uses one low-stakes unresolved situation. It creates a before/after record around four ordinary questions: what the participant notices, what they think it means, what decision appears available, and what action they may take.

This is not a claim that the sequence changes a person. It is a way to capture whether any difference, no difference, ambiguity, or breakdown is observable in one encounter.

## Intended mechanism to test

The candidate mechanism is deliberately narrow:

1. separate what is directly noticed from the meaning currently attached to it;
2. make room for one other plausible reading; and
3. name a small, reversible next action or an intentional choice not to act.

The mechanism is unverified until participant evidence is captured. A reported difference does not by itself establish causation.

## Encounter bounds

| Constraint | Bound |
| --- | --- |
| Participant | One consenting participant |
| Situation | One personally relevant, unresolved, low-stakes situation |
| Duration | 25 minutes planned; 30 minutes maximum |
| Delivery | One facilitator, the participant sheet, and a blank capture record |
| Participant knowledge | No WRASAL or WRAYvolution explanation required |
| Data boundary | No participant name, contact detail, or sensitive situation detail belongs in this repository |
| Outcome boundary | No participant outcome is assumed before capture |

Do not use an active crisis, imminent safety issue, medical emergency, legal emergency, or a situation requiring professional care. The participant may skip any prompt or stop at any time.

## Required packet

- **Participant-facing encounter:** `artifacts/BUILD-002-PARTICIPANT-ENCOUNTER.md`
- **Facilitator/system instructions and blank capture record:** `artifacts/BUILD-002-FACILITATOR-RUNSHEET.md`
- **Build record and event ledger:** `builds/BUILD-002-WRAYVOLUTION-ENCOUNTER.json`

The participant receives only the participant-facing document. The facilitator uses the runsheet. Neither document asks the participant to understand or evaluate WRASAL or WRAYvolution.

## Primary test

Can one participant complete the participant-facing encounter without an explanation of the underlying architecture?

Record, without judgment:

- whether the participant completed, skipped, or stopped;
- every request for clarification, using the participant's wording where practical;
- whether the facilitator only reread a prompt or had to add explanation; and
- elapsed time.

Needing extensive explanation, confusion about what to do, or stopping is valid evidence—not a reason to repair the record after the fact.

## Secondary test

Does the encounter yield directly captured information about any stated difference in:

- perception / what is noticed;
- interpretation / meaning attached to the situation;
- decision / option selected or withheld; and
- action / a chosen, declined, or later-reported next step?

The encounter may yield “no difference,” “unknown,” or ambiguous material. Those are valid results. It does not test or prove causation.

## Completion and decision rule

A complete participant run consists of the before, during, after, and follow-up capture fields being completed as far as the participant permits. The facilitator then classifies the material before interpreting it.

A participant’s liking the encounter is not a success criterion. The usable result is an accurate record of what happened, including a failure to complete or an absence of meaningful difference.

At the time this packet was built, **no participant has been run and no participant outcome is recorded**. The Build Mode decision therefore remains `BLOCKED` pending a real, consented encounter and captured evidence. The participant outcome remains **Unknown**.

Use an existing consented communication channel for the one follow-up. Do not implement any deferred capability as part of this build.
